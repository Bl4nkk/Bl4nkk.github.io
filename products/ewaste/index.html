Integrated modules

01

Collection & logistics

  • Pickup call registration with photos and map routing links
  • Transfer notes between units with batch integrity in transit
02

Procurement & inwarding

  • Lot, piece-rate or weight-based procurement
  • Goods receipt notes and purchase-bill export to Tally
03

Segregation & refurbishment

  • Lot-wise sorting into multiple output items
  • Identify usable stock, refurbish it and move it to finished goods
04

Dismantling & recycling

  • Condition tagging, material-group mapping and weight validation
  • Shredding or crushing processes, batch assignment and finished recycled stock
05

Sales & data destruction

  • Sell refurbished goods, spares and recycled outputs
  • Device-linked destruction records, certificates and chain-of-custody logs
06

Reports & add-ons

  • Yield, loss, cycle-time, inventory, transfer and statutory reports
  • Optional marketing, warehouse, accounts, HR, service and IT rental modules
